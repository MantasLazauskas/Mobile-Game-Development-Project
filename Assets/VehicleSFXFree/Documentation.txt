Vehicle SFX Free

Documentation. 30 sounds as WAV (48 kHz, 24-bit, mono), one AudioClip each, a folder
per kind:

  car/ (6)
    door_close_01, door_open_01, indicator_loop, keys_jingle_01, lock_chirp, trunk_close
  crashes/ (4)
    crash_car_01, crash_wall_01, metal_scrape_01, windscreen_shatter_01
  driving/ (8)
    backfire_01, car_accelerate, car_passby, car_rev, car_start, car_stop, motorbike_rev, truck_start
  engines/ (2)
    car_idle_loop, car_mid_loop
  horns/ (3)
    bicycle_bell_01, car_horn_short_01, truck_air_horn_01
  others/ (1)
    helicopter_loop
  sirens/ (1)
    police_wail_loop
  transit/ (2)
    air_brake_01, reverse_beeper_loop
  tyres/ (3)
    skid_long_01, skid_short_01, tyre_burst

  Demo/VehicleSFXFreeDemo.unity   a button per sound

Play one with AudioSource.PlayOneShot(clip). The WAVs import in any Unity version; the demo
scene needs Unity 6.3 or later.


Each file carries its type's prefix (T_ texture, M_ material, PF_ prefab, L_ scene, SH_ shader, AN_ clip,
AC_ controller, A_ audio, SM_ mesh, F_ font, DA_ data asset); this guide names files without it.
